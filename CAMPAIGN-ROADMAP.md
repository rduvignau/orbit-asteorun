# Asteorun — the completed eight-world arc

All eight chapters are implemented. The campaign has a beginning, finite continues, a final boss, an animated ending and a shared score archive. Every new expedition starts at level 1; there are no development checkpoints or level shortcuts in the interface.

## The last passage

Level 8 is 232 route units, longer than level 7's 210. Its continuous galleries preserve a readable path on phone, desktop and landscape layouts. Apertures move, capacitors release charge, and alternating currents announce their direction for three seconds before applying a steerable push. The Gate of Cinders guards the midpoint at 116 units.

The Dawn Engine is the largest encounter in the game. Its four 18-hit emitters each power a gun and release a charge cell when destroyed. The protected core then enters containment overload: locked amber beam lanes warn for 1.7 seconds before firing for 0.7 seconds. Breaking that 64-hit core exposes a moving 64-hit last heart, widens salvos and awakens the companion for the escape. Pulse remains viable through every phase. The armored right side remains hazardous, while all weak points are approachable from the left.

Victory lights a star rather than opening another level. The little ship and companion escape, the eclipse recedes, and a deliberately overstaffed arcade credit roll plays over drifting pieces of the human design prompts. Romaric Duvignau (10000 characters) occupies one human-design credit. ChatGPT Codex Sol 6.1 Medium occupies every other role. Final results offer the shared top ten, and a Hard victory unlocks Hardcore on that browser.

## Finished-game rules

Easy starts / respawns with 4 hearts and has 4 continues; Normal uses 3 and 3; Hard uses 2 and 2; Hardcore uses 1 and 1. Score multipliers are 0.75×, 1×, 1.5× and 2.5×. Repairs retain the five-heart cap. Continues restart the current chapter with its entry score and loadout, and never refill between chapters. Level transitions require player confirmation before a timed radio introduction. The final score records the last reached world and chosen difficulty in the shared server archive.
